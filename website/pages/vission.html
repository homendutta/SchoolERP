VISION

ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY believes that 'Innovation leads to Creation'. With this perception, ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY aims to bring a sea-change in the scenario of education by catering to the inner talents and interests of the students. ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY aims at producing quality human resources, who would be capable enough to contribute their share towards the dynamic fields in the society. We aim for the students to rise above the ordinary and transform them to reach their full potential in body, mind, and heart.

MISSION

ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY is pledged to transform its campus into an ideal educational hub. Hence, it offers an inspiring, dynamic, and student-friendly curriculum.

  • It targets an ideal student-teacher ratio in the classroom to meet individual needs and understand their abilities.
  • To give a better outlook and guidance in life, Career Counseling and Personality Development Sessions are introduced.
  • In order to make the teaching-learning process more dynamic, seminars, guest faculties, and experts from different fields will grace the Academy Campus.
  • Education is the birthright of all; hence, the Academy provides special provisions for physically challenged or specially blessed persons.
  • In order to actively participate in global communication, we offer Spoken English classes by experts within the campus.
  • To meet the needs of the present, in-house coaching facilities for different competitive exams and ITI training facilities are also available in the Academy.
  • In order to bring out the all-around development of the students, Sports & Games, and cultural activities are included in the curriculum.
  • ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY's goal is to equip every child with good virtue, attitude, and skills that would facilitate innovation and confidence in their contribution towards the dynamic global society without harming national sentiments and ethics.
