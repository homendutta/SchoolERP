ASOM JNANBHUMI
ACADEMY

'In search of a new horizon' with this motto ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY laid its foundation in the year 2019. It is a co-educational Junior College, which offers both Science and Arts stream affiliated to Assam Higher Secondary Education Council. It is situated at the educational zone of North Lakhimpur town- Khelmati, sharing its neighboring with the North Lakhimpur College (Autonomous). The structure of the college is aesthetically designed with modern architecture, which blends the true zeal of teaching and learning with positive vibes at everytime.

Committed to reach the height of excellence, ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY offers an upgraded and dynamic curriculum, which would encourage the students to bring forth their hidden potentials, inspire to pursue new interests and endow them with the craft needed to excel in the competitive 21st century. The Academy believes that every child is special and aims to nourish in thee so of any field, to bring the best them. So, along with the academics, sports, art or music and other such subjects are offered to foster the inherent-talents of individual.

The infrastructure of ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY is sophisticated as it has well equipped classrooms with modern furnishing, techno-friendly laboratories and a large open space in front to meet necessary needs and meets. Besides this the Academy also provides hostel facilities for both boys and girls within a minimum radius from the college building. The hostels have comfortable set up with homely environment and security provisions.

From its initial period, ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY has taken vow to achieve all round development of the child. It is dedicated to impart education through innovation and taste academic success in the truest sense

The highly qualified and experienced faculty members are the real treasure of ASOM JNANBHUMI ACADEMY. The skilled teachers are ever ready and enthusiast to shape the future of the students through their utmost dedication and sincerity. We ensure quality education to achieve the level of progress and success highest students and the Academy at large.
